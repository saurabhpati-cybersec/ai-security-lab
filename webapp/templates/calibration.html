🎚️ Detector calibration

Drag the threshold. TPR and FPR recompute live across the benign + 5 attack datasets — pure regex, sub-millisecond.

Calibration is the soul of detection engineering. Move the threshold up: fewer false alarms (lower FPR), but you miss more real attacks (lower TPR). Move it down: catch more attacks but break benign flows. There's no free lunch — pick the operating point your product can tolerate.
The curve looks like a staircase because the regex detector produces binary scores (0 or a fixed weight per rule). Scores cluster near 0.0 and 0.75–1.0; thresholds in the gap between those clusters don't change any classification.
