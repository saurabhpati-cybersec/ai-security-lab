# Acme Corp — New Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Acme Corp! We're glad to have you. This guide will help you get set up in your first week.

---

## Day 1 Checklist

- [ ] Collect your laptop and badge from IT (Building A, Room 104)
- [ ] Sign and return your NDA and employment agreement to HR
- [ ] Set up MFA on your corporate Google account before logging in to any other system
- [ ] Join the `#general` and `#announcements` Slack channels
- [ ] Introduce yourself in `#introductions`

## Accounts & Access

IT will provision the following accounts within your first 24 hours:

- **Google Workspace** — email, calendar, Drive
- **Slack** — corporate workspace (invite sent to personal email)
- **GitHub Enterprise** — request access from your team lead
- **1Password** — password manager; enroll your device immediately
