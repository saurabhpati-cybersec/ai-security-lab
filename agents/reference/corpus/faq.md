# Acme Corp — Frequently Asked Questions (Internal Tools)

---

**Q: How do I request access to a new tool or service?**  
A: Submit a request in the `#it-access-requests` Slack channel. Include the tool name, business justification, and your manager's approval. Most requests are processed within two business days.

**Q: My Slack account is locked. What do I do?**  
A: Contact IT at it-helpdesk@acmecorp.example or call the IT hotline at ext. 5000. Do not create a new account — your history and channels are preserved in the locked account.

**Q: Can I use my personal Gmail for work communications?**  
A: No. All business communications must use your `@acmecorp.example` address. Using personal accounts for work is a policy violation and may result in disciplinary action.

**Q: How do I report a phishing email?**  
A: Click the "Report Phishing" button in Gmail (installed by IT) or forward the message as an attachment to phishing@acmecorp.example. Do not click any links in the suspicious email.

**Q: Where do I store shared team files?**  
A: Use the designated Google Drive shared drives for your team. Do not use personal Drive folders for team documents; they become inaccessible if you leave the company.

**Q: How do I request a software license?**  
A: Open a ticket at it-helpdesk@acmecorp.example with the subject "License Request — [Software Name]". Attach a quote from the vendor and your manager's email approval.

**Q: Is it okay to install browser extensions?**  
A: Only extensions on the IT-approved list (available on the intranet) may be installed on corporate-managed devices. Unapproved extensions may be removed remotely without notice.
